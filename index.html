What We Do

Two ways we fill a seat.

Both put a qualified person on your program through a certified EDWOSB. One starts with a requisition you can't fill. The other starts with someone you have already picked.

LANE 01 — SOURCED

We find the person

You have an opening that isn't closing. We recruit nationally against that specific requisition, screen for real technical depth, and come back with people who can do the work.

  • Sourced nationally, remote or on-site.
  • Technical screening is run by a practising engineer who has done the work, not a recruiter matching keywords against a résumé.
  • Clearance level and investigation date verified before anyone is submitted.
  • A straight no when the req is outside what we can fill well.
LANE 02 — PAYROLLED

You found the person

You already have someone in mind and need the hours to run through a small business for subcontracting credit. You keep the selection. We carry the employment.

  • You pick the person. We stay out of it.
  • Employment, payroll, W-2, benefits and workers' comp all run through our employer of record.
  • Paperwork goes out the same day we have a name, a rate and a start date.
  • Conversion terms in writing at the start, so nobody is arguing about them at month six.
Why Take The Call

We count toward the goal you are measured on.

Primes carrying a subcontracting plan under FAR 19.7 get held to small-business participation targets, and the women-owned line is usually the one that comes up short at the end of the year. CDE Prosperity is certified by the SBA rather than self-attested, and carries WOSB, EDWOSB, small disadvantaged and minority-owned status at the same time. All of it is verifiable in SAM.gov under UEI SJHNYDQL94Q7.

On set-aside work where you self-perform, similarly situated small-business labor works in your favour instead of against you.

  • A yes or a no inside 24 hours. Every requisition, including the ones we decline.
  • No filler. If there is one good person for the seat, you get one, not three padded out to look responsive.
  • Screened by an engineer. Technical screening is done by someone who has held the job, which is rare at our size and it is the reason our submissions are short.
  • DoD-wide and remote-first. Where the seat sits is usually not a problem.
Labor Categories & Clearances

What we source.

Software, systems, engineering and technical program-support roles under the NAICS and PSC registrations we hold. We recruit against live requisitions rather than keeping a speculative bench, which is why the honest yes-or-no comes first.

Software Engineer DevSecOps Engineer Systems Engineer Cloud & Infrastructure Engineer Data Engineer Database Administrator Cybersecurity Analyst Systems Administrator ITSM & Service Desk Test & Integration Engineer Technical Writer Program & Project Support

Requisitions outside these categories are still worth sending. We will tell you inside 24 hours whether we can serve them properly, rather than taking them on and going quiet.

Clearance posture

We place professionals who already hold current clearances, and our present lane is Public Trust and CUI-level work. We do not hold a facility clearance. If FCL sponsorship would open a requirement to us, that is a conversation we want to have.

For cleared professionals

We are small, so you talk to the people making the decisions. The technical screen is run by a working engineer, and our overhead is low enough that more of the bill rate reaches you. We reach out against real openings, and we don't go silent on candidates.
